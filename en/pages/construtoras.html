The problem

The risk arises at the time of sale. The problem only becomes apparent when the contract is transferred.

When a construction company allows the down payment to be paid in installments without properly assessing the buyer’s creditworthiness, it may bear the risk for months until it discovers that the buyer is unable to secure bank financing.

24months

Until the results reveal the failure.

You paid the down payment in installments, received payments for two years, and when it came time to transfer the funds, the bank reviewed the case according to its own criteria and denied the request. The sale falls through at the end of the process, not at the beginning.

1unit

Returns to inventory with each cancellation.

Money refunded, commission already paid, two years of development, and a unit that needs to be resold—now that the project is more mature.

0%

The financing commission is up to you.

The buyer finalizes the loan with the bank manager. The origination fee—which is revenue—becomes someone else’s commission—and you lose control over the timeline of the sale itself.

The core of Credituz

Increase your chances of securing financing through the transfer before taking on the risk.

Use your own policies, credit bureau data, payment capacity, and credit indicators to better select which borrowers to include in your portfolio. The final decision on financing remains with the bank, but your operation provides the bank with much more context and control when the loan is passed on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the analysis prevent contract termination during the transfer?+

The dealership applies its credit policy and checks credit bureaus at the time of sale, before you finance the down payment. Instead of finding out that the application was denied two years later, you’ll already know on the day of the offer whether that buyer will be approved for financing.

Can I write my own credit policy?+

Yes—that’s the core of the product. You set the minimum income, maximum loan-to-income ratio, down payment, eligible borrower profile, and any other rules you want. In Enterprise, policies can vary by company and by product.

What types of credit lines do you originate?+

Real estate financing, construction loans, home equity loans, and auto equity loans—with loan calculators and application processing through the country’s largest financial institutions, all in a single consultation.

Does this work for high VGV and tickets ranging from R$ 500,000 to R$ 2 million?+

Yes. The workflow was designed for real estate financing. The more complex the financing, the more a preliminary analysis protects the sale and speeds up the closing.

Will it replace my current ERP or CRM (Sienge, CV)?+

You can either replace your existing system or use it alongside it. Credituz acts as an AI layer on top of your ERP, with real-time, two-way synchronization, and imports contacts with a single click. Many construction companies are making the switch gradually.

Does the business plan replace my spreadsheet?+

Yes. Units, reservations, contract status, and availability by category are all stored in a single source that sales, operations, and finance can view simultaneously.

Is the admission fee charged automatically?+

Yes. In Credituz OS, the workflow automates up to 300 billings per month via WhatsApp and email, with up to three configurable dates per billing. SMS and additional workflows can be included in Enterprise projects.

How long will it take to go live?+

Active account in 5 minutes, self-service. Integration with your ERP (API token, field mapping, and activation) takes up to 24 hours. No 60-day implementation period.
