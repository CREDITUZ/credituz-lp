Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tenant screening process work?+

The system checks credit bureaus and calculates payment capacity based on the criteria you set—minimum income, maximum debt-to-income ratio, and accepted collateral. The decision is made instantly, with the results displayed on the screen, rather than relying on an insurance company’s review.

Does it replace surety insurance or a guarantor?+

Credituz is not an insurance company: it provides you with the analysis you need to make an informed decision. Many real estate agencies use the platform to filter leads in advance and reduce claims, while maintaining the coverage they already have in place.

Can I write my own approval policy?+

Yes—it’s the core of the product, and it applies to both sales and rentals. In Enterprise, policies may vary by company and by product.

Do I earn a commission on my client's loan?+

Yes. When you originate a loan through the platform, 40% of the commission paid by the bank goes to your business on the Credituz OS plan, and 75% on the Enterprise plan.

Does it integrate with the real estate portals (ZAP, OLX, VivaReal)?+

Yes, it's already included in the plan, with real-time two-way sync. You can also import leads and contacts with just one click.

Is rent collected automatically?+

Yes. The system automates billing via WhatsApp and email at R$ 0.15 per transmission, including payment slips, Pix, write-offs, reconciliation, NFe, and transfers to the property owner. SMS is available exclusively in the Enterprise plan, on a custom basis.

Is it suitable for a small real estate agency with just a few agents?+

Yes. The Credituz OS costs R$ 297 per month, supports 3 users, has no expensive setup fees, and has no minimum contract term for the monthly fee.

How long will it take to go live?+

Active account in 5 minutes, self-service. Integration with portals and your system takes up to 24 hours. No 60-day implementation period.
