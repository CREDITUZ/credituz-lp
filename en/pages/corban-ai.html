What is a credit pipeline?

The credit pipeline is an overview of operations from simulation through to contract execution. It displays the stage, organization, documents, pending items, person in charge, next action, and status for each request. In CORBAN AI, this pipeline is housed in the Corban CRM so that sales and operations teams can work within the same context.

For analysis, internal policy, credit management, and collections, use the Credituz OS. To run a simulation, place an order, or track an ATM transaction, use CORBAN AI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CORBAN AI replace a sales CRM?

It was not designed as a generic CRM. Its focus is the credit process: simulation, application, documents, pending items, banks, status, and contract execution.

Is it possible to combine CORBAN AI and Credituz OS?

Yes. They are standalone products and can be combined into an Enterprise solution.

Is Credituz the one providing the financing?

No. Credituz coordinates the process and operates through authorized partners. The final review, approval, and granting of the loan are the responsibility of the financial institutions.
