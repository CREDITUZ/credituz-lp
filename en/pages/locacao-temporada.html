Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from those who manage leases.

Does it work for both long-term and seasonal use at the same time? +

Yes. AI-powered customer service, contracts, and recurring billing are currently available for both models. Automatic synchronization with Airbnb, Booking, and Google Hotels (calendar and rates) is still under development, and that’s what the priority list above is for.

Will it replace the rental system I'm already using? +

For most operations, yes: CRM, customer service, contracts, and billing are all handled in Credituz. You can run both systems in parallel during the migration, importing contacts and properties with a single click, and keep whichever one you prefer.

Is there an extra charge for recurring billing? +

The billing schedule, payment slip, and Pix are included in the Enterprise plan, which includes an Asaas digital account. The NFe costs R$ 0.49 per invoice. There are no charges per property or per tenant.

When will the integration with the OTAs be ready? +

It is currently under active development. Those who join the priority list will receive release dates first, gain early access, and qualify for special launch terms. Fill out the form above and we’ll get in touch.
