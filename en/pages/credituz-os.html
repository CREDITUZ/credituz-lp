Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a charge?

A billing cycle is a complete set of billing statements, not a single billing statement. It can have up to three configurable dates and use WhatsApp and email.

Can I purchase just one analysis?

Yes. Credituz Score offers a one-time consultation for R$ 27.70, with no monthly fee.

Does Credituz OS offer credit?

No. The product supports analysis, internal decision-making, and operational management. Financing is provided by authorized partner institutions.
