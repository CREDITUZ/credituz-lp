Frequently Asked Questions About the Service

What is the Credituz Service Plan?

This is the plan in which Credituz, in addition to providing the infrastructure, does the work: analysis, decision-making, collections, and portfolio monitoring are carried out by AI agents under the supervision of our team, through Credituz OS Growth. The company sets the policy and goals and receives the results.

What is software-as-a-service?

It’s the idea of selling the work that’s been done rather than just selling the tool. Instead of a company purchasing software and putting together a team to operate it, it contracts for a turnkey operation, delivered with AI and human supervision.

What can be included in the Service?

Analysis and billing operations via Credituz, customized users and volumes, AI-powered contracts, digital signatures, digital notary services, SMS, API, integrations, white-label solutions, SSO, multi-company support, AI-specific solutions, and SLAs with dedicated support.

Does the service have a fixed price?

No. The scope and price are determined based on the operation, volumes, integrations, security requirements, and service level, with an annual contract.

Does the company lose control over decisions?

No. The credit policy, approval criteria, and collection targets are set by the company. Credituz operates within these rules, records the reason for each decision, and refers cases that require judgment to the team.

Is it possible to keep the ERP and CRM systems up to date?

Yes. Depending on the project, the Credituz can operate as a layer integrated with existing systems or centralize specific workflows.
