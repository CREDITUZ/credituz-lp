CORBAN AI

It's not just software. It's a banking correspondent that approves and secures the financing.

A banking correspondent, or CORBAN, is someone authorized to originate and arrange loans with banks—including real estate financing, construction loans, home equity loans, and auto equity loans. X1’s CORBAN AI combines this process with artificial intelligence: it runs simulations across multiple financial institutions, puts together the proposal, approves, and signs the loan—all without your client ever having to leave the purchasing journey.

Financial Management

He sold it and financed it. Now the money just comes in on its own.

Once the contract is signed, the part that slows down most operations begins: collecting payments every month, reconciling payments received, issuing invoices, tracking transfers, and identifying who is behind on payments. Credituz handles this entire cycle—from issuing the payment slip to automatic write-off—without spreadsheets or a collections team.

Accounts Receivable and Portfolio

Each contract is displayed as a portfolio showing installments, due dates, interest, and adjustments. Monthly payments, in-house financing, and third-party financing are all shown on the same screen, with a consolidated view by project and by customer.

Multichannel Billing Rules

The system automatically sends notifications via WhatsApp, SMS, and email, in the order and at the intervals you set. The AI crafts the message in the right tone for each stage: a reminder before the due date, a notice when payment is overdue, and a follow-up negotiation afterward.

Receipt, Write-Off, and Reconciliation

The payment slip, Pix, and payment link are included in the message itself. The payment is credited to the digital account, the installment is automatically marked as paid, and the reconciliation takes place without anyone having to check the statement.

NFe, pass-through and delinquency

An invoice issued for each payment received, bank transfers tracked until funds are released, and a dashboard showing delinquencies, portfolio aging, and cash flow projections for the coming months.

View calculation assumptions

Methodology: We start with the sales you’re already making and estimate how many of them will be financed within your operation. Based on the financed amount (assumed to be 70% of the receipt), we apply the average commission that the bank pays to the correspondent (1%) and, on top of that, its share: 40% on the R$297 plan and 60% on the Enterprise plan. In practice, its revenue is 0.4% of the financed amount in the plan and 0.6% at Enterprise.

Financed sales within the transaction:

Calculation: sales × % financed × (average transaction amount × 70%) × 1% commission × your share = monthly revenue.

Reference estimate. The actual commission varies by bank, product, buyer profile, and transaction volume, and is specified in the contract with the financial institution. It does not constitute a contractual guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is this useful for people who aren't in the real estate industry?

+
It works. Credituz is a credit and billing operating system: any business that offers financing or collects recurring payments uses the entire platform. Construction companies screen buyers before financing a down payment; property managers and landlords collect rent automatically via automated billing, payment slips, Pix, and NFe; clinics, schools, and service providers with monthly fees automate collections and reconciliation. The core functions—credit assessment, offering financing, collecting payments, and reconciling accounts—are the same across all of them.

Will it work with my current CRM?

+
Yes. Credituz imports contacts from your current CRM with just one click and connects to ERPs and CRMs such as Sienge, CV CRM, Kenlo, and Conaz, with real-time, two-way synchronization. You can use it in parallel while migrating or let Credituz completely replace your current system. That’s what most people end up doing.

How much does it cost to get started?

+
R$ 297 per month. It’s a single plan that includes the entire platform: CRM, AI agents, multi-bank simulation, contracts, a sales portal on your own domain, and credit origination. Sign-up is self-service, and you can cancel anytime directly within the platform.

How does Credituz make money?

+
Two sources of revenue: (1) the monthly plan fee, and (2) the commission from partner banks when a loan originated on the platform is approved. This commission is split with you: you receive 40% on the R$ 297 plan and 60% on the Enterprise plan. Alignment of interests: we only earn when you close a deal.

Is AI good at Portuguese?

+
Yes, she is trained in Brazilian credit and collections terminology: she understands pay stubs, FGTS statements, vehicle registration numbers, CDC, lease agreements, and the terms that appear in a loan offer. Reports and collection messages are written in natural Portuguese, without technical jargon.

Will my team be able to use it?

+
Setup in 5 minutes, an interface based on the WhatsApp/Notion standard (which everyone is already familiar with), and no scheduled training. In addition to the knowledge base and video tutorials, the plan includes live chat support Monday through Friday, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and the ability to open support tickets directly within the platform. The Enterprise plan includes a dedicated customer service representative.
